Dave Cummings

I've been building websites professionally since 2008, mostly for ecommerce businesses that needed to move fast and stay found in search. The better part of a decade went to Magento and Shopify implementations for mid-sized retailers. That work taught me one thing clearly: performance and architecture decisions made at launch determine what's painful to fix three years later.

At some point I got more interested in the businesses themselves than the platforms. Small businesses have the same problems as large ones, less margin for error, and no internal team to absorb the cost of a bad build. A custom site that loads in under two seconds and shows up in AI-generated recommendations does more work for a ten-person company than for a mid-market retailer with a paid search budget. That's where I focus now.

The way I work has changed over the last two years. AI tools are part of the process, which means I can scope and ship sites faster than the traditional agency model without sacrificing quality on the things that matter. I'm not using AI to generate boilerplate. I'm using it to compress the research and planning phases so I can spend more time on structure, performance, and making sure the site actually works for real customers and real search queries.

I'm based in Round Rock, Texas, about 20 miles north of Austin. I work directly with clients, no account managers, no handoff to a junior team. If you hire me, you work with me. That's not an accident.
