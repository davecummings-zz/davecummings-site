Dave Cummings

I've been building websites professionally since 2008, early on for businesses and non-profits that needed to move fast and get online. I started out leaning on content management systems, which made it easy to build a site and hand over the keys to someone else to maintain and edit. Those projects taught me the real tradeoffs behind architecture and performance decisions, and how much of the pain comes later when technology moves and the site can't keep up.

Helping people work through these challenges got me more interested in the businesses themselves than in wrestling with platform limitations. Small businesses have the same problems as large ones, less margin for error, and no internal team to absorb the cost of decisions that turned out to be wrong. A custom site that loads in under two seconds and shows up in AI-generated recommendations does more work for a ten-person company than for a mid-market retailer with a paid search budget. That's where I focus now.

I spent most of the last decade at an ecommerce SaaS platform, where I grew from developer to solutions engineering manager. That meant staying close to code while building out and mentoring teams. Being a player-coach is what I do best.

The way I work has changed over the last two years. AI tools are increasingly part of the process, which means I can scope and ship sites faster than the traditional agency model without sacrificing quality on the things that matter. I'm not using AI to generate boilerplate. I'm using it to compress the research and planning phases so I can spend more time on structure, performance, and making sure the site actually works for real customers and real search queries. I enjoy working in React, Next.js, TypeScript, and Vercel.

I'm based in Central Texas, just north of Austin. I work directly with clients, no account managers, no handoff to a junior team. If you hire me, you work with me. That's how I like it.
